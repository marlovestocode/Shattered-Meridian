# Project Vision

## What Shattered Meridian is

A heavy-PvP Roblox RPG set in a fractured eastern-mythology world, built around cultivation
(qi) progression that is earned exclusively through fighting other players. No idle farming,
no PvE grind as a path to power. Every meaningful gain — Meridian XP, art mastery, absorbed
essence — comes from a real fight against a real opponent. This is the project's single
non-negotiable pillar: **power is earned in combat, not accumulated around it.**

The intended feel sits between Sekiro-grade combat precision (lock-on, parry, posture,
punishing but readable) and a cultivation-novel power fantasy (visible tiers, bloodlines,
forbidden arts, corruption as a cost of power). It is not a battle-royale, not an idle game,
and not a tab-target MMO.

## Target bar

Shattered Meridian is being built to be one of the highest-quality PvP RPGs on Roblox, developed
over years, not a weekend prototype. Every design and engineering decision should be judged
against "does this hold up under years of live updates and a competitive playerbase," not
"does this work for a demo."

## Pillars (in priority order)

1. **Fight-to-grow.** Progression systems that can be satisfied without fighting another player
   are bugs, not features, unless explicitly scoped as a minor QoL exception.
2. **Readable power.** A stronger player should look, sound, and feel stronger at a glance —
   tier, bloodline, and art choices are supposed to be legible to an opponent, not hidden stats.
3. **Weight and consequence.** Corruption, Qi Deviation, and faction standing exist so that power
   is never free. Systems that let players stack strength with zero downside undermine this pillar.
4. **Skill expression over gear checks.** Combat outcomes should be decided more by execution
   (parry timing, spacing, art sequencing) than by raw stat gaps, especially in close tiers.
5. **A world worth remembering.** Regions, factions, and lore should feel like a coherent
   mythology someone could read a wiki page about, not reskinned generic fantasy.

## Audience

Players who like Soulslike combat tension and enjoy long-term character-building — the kind of
player who reads patch notes and cares about tier lists. Not designed to be casual-first; systems
can have depth and a learning curve, but the core combat loop (lock-on, block, parry, attack)
must still be immediately playable by a new player in their first fight.

## Tone guardrails

- Eastern mythology-inspired, original mythology — not a reskin of a specific IP (wuxia/xianxia
  influence, not a retelling of any single existing work).
- Grim but not edgy-for-its-own-sake: corruption and deviation are consequences of ambition, not
  shock value.
- No comic relief factions or joke systems — the tone is serious high fantasy throughout.

## Decision filter

When a proposed feature is ambiguous, weigh it against the pillars above in order. If it
weakens fight-to-grow or makes power illegible, it needs rework even if it's mechanically fun in
isolation. This file outranks every other reference file when there's a genuine conflict.
