# World Bible

This is canon. Treat every entry here as binding continuity — new lore must extend it without
contradicting it. If a request would contradict something below, flag the conflict before
writing new material.

## The Shattering

The founding cataclysm of the setting. The world's original unified qi meridian network — the
"Meridian" the game is named for — was torn apart in a war whose full cause is deliberately
left as a mystery to be doled out through discoverable lore, not exposition-dumped. Its
aftermath left three fractured inheritances of power (Celestial, Demonic, and the unclaimed
Unbound path) and scarred the world into distinct regions, each carrying a different flavor of
the damage.

## Factions

- **Celestial (Firmament Sect).** Inheritors of ordered, disciplined qi — dragon-vein lineages,
  hierarchy, martial formality. Their bloodlines and arts favor precision and control.
- **Demonic.** Inheritors of aggressive, corrupting qi — power that grows faster but carries
  Corruption risk. Not "evil" in a cartoonish sense; a philosophy of strength through embracing
  what the Celestial path forbids.
- **Unbound.** Those outside both sects, including Humans who awaken bloodlines that were never
  supposed to belong to them. Contested authority rather than inherited authority is the emotional
  core of this faction — earning power the world says you shouldn't have.

Faction identity should read through mechanics, not just flavor text: Celestial kits reward
patience and setup, Demonic kits reward risk-taking and punish caution, Unbound kits are the
most build-flexible but the least forgiving of mistakes.

## Races

Four races exist. Full write-ups (identity, passives, actives, the Human Ascension gate) live in
`progression-systems.md` since they're mechanically load-bearing — this file is the place for
their cultural/lore identity, that file is the place for their kit.

## Bloodlines

Thirteen bloodlines exist, each with a rarity tier, an awakening condition, a flavor identity,
and staged unlocks. Full mechanical stage-by-stage breakdowns live in `progression-systems.md`;
this file governs their *lore* — what they mean in-world, who can plausibly carry one, and how
awakening one should read narratively (e.g., a Human awakening a dragon-vein bloodline like
Tianlong is framed as contested authority, not inherited birthright — the world noticing someone
who refused to be ordinary).

## Regions

Three regions currently exist, each tied to a different post-Shattering wound:

- **The Void** — ethereal, reality-fragmenting nothingness. Corruption-stack mechanics live here
  environmentally, not just as a player system.
- **The Median Paradise** — lush, contested heartland; the closest thing to neutral ground and
  the region most factions actually fight over.
- **The Demonic Disastrous Landscape** — hellish, chaos-zone terrain with active environmental
  hazards (geysers, corruption creep, terrain takeover).

Full geography, landmarks, and hazard specs live in `world-design.md`; this file is for what
each region *means* thematically and how it maps onto the Shattering's aftermath.

## The Meridian Particle

The setting's core in-world physics concept: a fragment of the original unified Meridian that
persists in every qi-user, dormant until awakened. It is the narrative justification for
bloodline awakening, Ascension, and Qi Deviation — keep any new power-granting mechanic
traceable back to this concept rather than inventing an unrelated power source.

## Glossary

Maintain and extend this list as new canon terms are introduced. Keep definitions to one or two
sentences — this is a lookup table, not an essay.

- **Qi** — the setting's fundamental life/power energy; see `progression-systems.md` for qi types.
- **Meridian** — the original unified qi network, shattered at the setting's founding event.
- **Ascension** — the gate through which a Human can claim power outside their birth race.
- **Corruption** — accumulated cost of Demonic-leaning or forbidden power use.
- **Qi Deviation** — a failure state from qi misuse or overreach; mechanics in `progression-systems.md`.
- **Unfurling** — a cultivator turning their own Meridian Particle inside out, so that for a few seconds the
  ground around them runs on their meridian's law — a fragment of the unified Meridian restored under one
  will. The rarest ultimate a fighter can hold; mechanics in `docs/design/domains.md`.

## Extending this file

New lore additions go under the section they belong to (a new region under Regions, a new
faction sub-group under Factions, etc.). Don't create parallel "lore v2" sections — integrate.
When in doubt about whether something is lore (goes here) or a mechanic (goes in
`progression-systems.md`), lore is "what it means," mechanics is "what it does to numbers."
